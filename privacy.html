Privacy Policy

Willagrams · Last updated 19 September 2026

Willagrams is a word game. This policy explains exactly what it stores and why. It is short because the app collects very little.

There is no account to sign up for

Willagrams never asks for an email address, a password, a phone number or your real name. When you first open online play, the app creates an anonymous identifier for your device automatically. That identifier is how your profile and friends list are recognised — nothing about it is tied to your identity.

What is stored

  • An anonymous account identifier — a random value generated on your device.
  • A display name — the one you choose. Other players can see it.
  • A friend code — eight characters, generated for you, so friends can find you.
  • Your friends list — who you have added, and pending or blocked requests.
  • Game statistics — matches played, matches won, tiles placed, and your fastest win.
  • Match data — the settings and final board of games you play online, so both players see the same result.

What is never collected

  • Your location.
  • Your contacts, photos, calendar, microphone or camera.
  • Your email address, real name or phone number.
  • Advertising identifiers. There is no advertising in Willagrams.
  • Analytics or usage tracking of any kind. The app contains no analytics or advertising software.

Tracking

Willagrams does not track you. Nothing it stores is linked to data from other companies' apps or websites, and nothing is shared with data brokers or advertisers. Your data is never sold.

Who else can see it

Game data is stored with Supabase, which hosts the database and the real-time connection that online matches run over. Supabase processes this data on our behalf and for no other purpose. No other third party receives it.

Other players can see your display name, your friend code if you give it to them, and the statistics shown on your profile. Your friends list is visible only to you and to the person at the other end of each friendship.

How long it is kept

Your profile and statistics are kept until they are deleted. Because the account is anonymous and lives on one device, deleting the app or moving to a new device means the data can no longer be reached and is no longer used.

Deleting your data

Email the address below from the device in question and ask, and the profile, friends list and statistics attached to your friend code will be deleted. Please include your friend code so the right record is found.

Children

Willagrams is suitable for all ages and collects nothing beyond what is listed above from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
