Willagrams

Race to build your word grid.

Willagrams is a word game for iPhone and iPad. Draw letter tiles, build them into a connected grid of your own, and empty your hand before your opponent empties theirs.

Support

Found a bug, stuck on something, or want a feature? Email nseluga@g.hmc.edu and you will get a reply.

It helps to include your device (iPhone or iPad), your iOS version, and your friend code if the problem involves playing with someone else.

Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-up, no email and no password. Online play creates an anonymous profile for you automatically.
  • How do I play with a friend? Open Play a Friend and share the invite. Add people using their eight-character friend code from the Friends screen.
  • I moved to a new phone and my profile is gone. Profiles are anonymous and live on one device, so they do not transfer. Email the address above if you need the old one cleared.
  • Can I play on my own? Yes — Solo Practice plays against the computer at Easy, Medium or Hard.

Privacy

Willagrams has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
