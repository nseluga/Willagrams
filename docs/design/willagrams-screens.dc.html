How to play
1 OF 2
POOL
One shared supply
Both players draw from the same face-down Pool of {{ poolSize }} tiles.
DRAW
Place your last tile
Your grid must be one connected cluster with no unknown words. Then everyone takes one.
SWAP
One back, three out
Return a tile you cannot use and take three. Needs three left in the Pool.
INVALID
What holds the Draw
Loose tiles are legal to leave lying around — they just block the Draw until they join up.
Call Willagrams! when your rack is empty and the Pool is dry.
